# The Trading City of Oakhaven



Oakhaven is the largest port city on the south coast and is considered the gateway to the sea routes.



## The Seafarers’ Guild

The city is effectively governed by the councilors of the Seafarers’ Guild. They control the entire fishing industry as well as the export of tropical fruits and spices.



## Religions

The city’s main temple is dedicated to the sea god *Aegaeon*. Fishermen and merchants offer libations there before every voyage.
