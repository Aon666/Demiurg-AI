# The Misty Mountains and Their Treasures


The Misty Mountains stretch across the entire northern part of the continent. Their peaks are shrouded in thick cloud mist year-round.


## Deposits and Mining

In the deep tunnels beneath the mountain ranges, the dwarves of the Grimm clan mine rare **obsidian** and **mithril**. These minerals are the basis for crafting magical focus crystals and runic armor.


## Climate and Dangers

The mountain passes are impassable in winter. In addition to frost giants, packs of ogres roam the area in search of prey.
